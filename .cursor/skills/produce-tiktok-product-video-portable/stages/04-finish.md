## Finish sequentially

1. Revalidate the actual payload and approved execution plan before any credit-consuming action.
2. Apply centered, prominent, visually wrapped captions with the case editor's caption program (ChatCut Caption Cards or CapCut native captions). Do not use Motion Graphics as the viewer-facing caption layer. On ChatCut, apply the saved user caption preset `product-video-center` once (`edit_captions` `preset_apply`) instead of restyling from scratch. That preset is Dela Gothic One, white fill, 14px black stroke, drop shadow, highlight off, and no background band. Caption Cards style, font, stroke, and `preset_save` / `preset_apply` do not consume ChatCut generation credits. After custom cards exist, do not write `maxLines`, `maxCharactersPerLine`, or `pacing`, and do not `refresh`: those rematerialize cards and can duplicate CJK text. Official CapCut text templates are optional. For CapCut Web, a named official text template may be applied and read back when it is actually used. For a host editor adapter in this same case, apply host-native captions without claiming a CapCut resource read-back. Never HOLD because an official CapCut template was not applied. Composed viewer pixels beat caption JSON geometry.
3. Remove or replace each task-owned rough caption so exactly one visible final caption remains per cut. Opacity zero is not removal proof. Place those captions at screen center. Do not add a caption background band. If a frozen line overflows the width, wrap it visually at an existing punctuation or phrase boundary without changing characters. If that card still clips after wrap, shrink **that card only** (for example `sizePx` 50) with `set_card_style`; do not change other cards, do not change wording, and do not regenerate TTS. That per-card size is a card exception, not a field of `product-video-center`. Keep spoken TTS text as the frozen line. The canonical final tail must keep a matching centered hold after TTS ends through `timeline_end_frame`. ChatCut Caption Cards follow ASR words on their speech item; `cue_override` success JSON is not a hold. Do not clone the same TTS asset as a muted hold to extend cards. If that hold cannot be proven, stop with `HOLD_CAPTION_TAIL_NOT_CLOSED`. Do not request `完成・書き出しOK` from this skill. If adjacent caption cards share a bulk-ASR token at a half-open boundary, reset the neighbors before rewriting the middle card. Do not refresh captions while a caption-hold audio track is muted.
4. For each narration `cut_id` in order, verify the frozen TTS input. After `粗編集OK`, generate official Holiday Twist without asking Path 1 (substitute voice) or Path 2 (new CapCut case). Generate one official Holiday Twist render per narration-target cut: paste only that cut's frozen line. Do not paste every frozen line into one bulk render, and do not insert blank-line scene separators as generation input. If the editor of record cannot emit that CapCut official preset, use the TTS sidecar immediately: official CapCut Text to Speech, one frozen line per render, no picture import. After each result card exists, prove it speaks only that frozen line, then capture its media URL (`video`/`audio` currentSrc, typically `mime_type=audio_mpeg`) into the case TTS working directory with `${SKILL_ROOT}/scripts/capture_capcut_result_audio.py`. CapCut keeps leftover textarea text; mixed later lines are a defective initial render, not a third generate. Do not click オーディオのみ, do not use Finder/OS/embedded save dialogs, do not wait for `~/Downloads/CapCut_TTS_*`, and do not ask the operator to press Save. If a save dialog appears, dismiss or ignore it and capture the result-card bytes instead. Do not regenerate that successful cut, and do not click さらに編集. Import that audio working copy into the editor of record as that cut's TTS clip. Official Holiday Twist audio may also be generated once per cut on CapCut Web when that is the editor of record. Immediately read back clip identity, credit result, text, official preset, common speed, start/end, containment, and adjacency. Each successful per-cut render consumes that `cut_id`'s initial generation slot. Do not also run a bulk render of every line. Do not use `${SKILL_ROOT}/scripts/prepare_bulk_tts_scene_gaps.py` on the routine path. Place one TTS clip per narration-target caption. Three-layer ends follow that cut's own audible speech end. Do not leave one combined narration clip on the timeline.
5. Use only the plan's per-cut single same-input repair when the initial result is definitively defective or absent. Never use a third attempt or change frozen inputs.
6. After every valid TTS clip exists, read the actual audible speech end for each cut. For every non-final cut, remove task-owned TTS trailing silence when present, then align the source clip, caption clip, and TTS clip to the same start and end frames; the next cut's three layers must start at that same shared end. Adjust only derived video playback speed, placement, and boundaries while preserving the frozen source asset, source in/out, visible action, text, and voice identity. Never repair only TTS, only source/caption, or any other two-layer subset. Do not request another checkpoint. Keep the configured canonical final visual at full duration and extend its caption through the full tail; when needed, split the canonical source at the final TTS end and keep the remainder as the tail segment.
7. Reconcile source, caption, narration target, and TTS by `cut_id`, including exact non-final start/end equality across all three timeline layers. Preserve verified cuts while repairing one failing cut. Write and validate `product_video_nonfinal_slack_receipt.v1` with `${SKILL_ROOT}/scripts/validate_nonfinal_slack.py <receipt> --project-root <task-root>`. For an explicitly approved `narration: none` payload, record `narration_target: false`, null TTS/audible/slack fields, and null playback audio/ASR bindings while still closing source/caption continuity and the final visual/caption tail.
8. Before handing off to final QA, inspect every cut rather than only changed cuts: prove the expected source clip still exists, its audio uses exact track/clip mute (`muted: true`, null gain; prefer picture-lane track mute) rather than gain reduction, exactly one caption remains visible through each cut's last valid frame (final cut through `timeline_end_frame`), and the source renders non-black at the first valid frame, midpoint, and last valid frame. A caption/TTS pair without its source clip is an unfinished timeline even when all boundary numbers match.
