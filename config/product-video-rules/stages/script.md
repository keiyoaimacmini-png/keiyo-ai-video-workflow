# Script-stage rules

Write scripts for TikTok mobile short-video dynamics and viewer retention:

1. **Short, punchy pacing**:
   - Keep non-final cuts brief and fast-paced (typically around 2.0 seconds per cut, maximum 3.0 seconds).
   - Cut 1 must act as a strong immediate hook within the first 0.5–1.0 second.

2. **Dopamine-focused, colloquial wording**:
   - Use high-impact colloquial wording, direct viral hooks, and intuitive benefit phrasing (e.g., "マジで地獄すぎ", "ガチで神アイテム", "パッと一瞬！", "隙間なく完全ガード！", "直射日光カットで超快適！").
   - Avoid slow corporate explanations or monotone descriptive prose.
   - Keep character counts tight so speech fits naturally within the short cut duration at base or slight speedup.

3. **Strict adherence to verified facts**:
   - While the tone is colloquial and energetic, every claim must map directly to verified product facts in the closed manifest.
   - Do not exaggerate beyond verified features or make unobserved claims.
   - Preserve the 6-stage progression: `problem_or_hook -> product -> use_or_change -> result -> problem_resolution -> cta`.
   - The final CTA line must remain the exact settings text: `下からチェック！`.
